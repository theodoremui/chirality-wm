# What the 44 source papers establish

This review follows the original numbering so each claim can be traced to its paper. Each entry identifies the research problem, the closest line of work, the design or assumptions behind the result, and its consequence for a proposal. The sources include theory, surveys, empirical studies and preprints. We consulted full texts, including the publisher PDF for SwapPF. Section and theorem numbers refer to the linked version. The experiments in these papers are reported by their authors.

## Equivariance and learned dynamics

**01.** **Cohen & Welling, Group Equivariant Convolutional Networks (ICML 2016), §§4–8.** Can an image network reuse a feature in several orientations as conventional convolution reuses it at translated positions? Group correlation and subgroup pooling give explicit transformation laws on discrete grids. The authors compare rotation/reflection groups, ordinary networks and augmentation on rotated MNIST and CIFAR, including capacity controls. Better classification and mathematically guaranteed feature transport support parameter sharing when the task respects the chosen group. Proposals 1 and 2 ask how these constraints affect action choice or track recovery when earlier evidence can be lost. [Proceedings and paper](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v48/cohenc16.html)

**02.** **Bronstein, Bruna, Cohen & Veličković, Geometric Deep Learning: Grids, Groups, Graphs, Geodesics, and Gauges (2021 survey), §§3.1–3.5, 4.5 and 5.7–5.8.** Why can architectures on grids, sets, groups and manifolds reuse the same design principles? The survey derives a common template from symmetry, stability and scale separation, relating group convolutions, message passing and recurrent updates. It distinguishes a physical transformation from changing the coordinates used to describe a quantity. The template assumes a specified domain and action. The concrete transfer to Proposal 2 is to state separate actions for mirroring the body and renaming its detector outputs. This is a framework and synthesis, with illustrated applications. [Full survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2104.13478)

**03.** **Yeh, Hu & Schwing, Chirality Nets for Human Pose Regression (NeurIPS 2019), Fig. 1 and §§3–4.** Can pose regression exploit knowledge that reflecting a skeleton exchanges anatomical labels? Compared with flip augmentation, constrained weights and biases enforce this law through feedforward, convolutional and recurrent layers. Pose lifting, pose forecasting and action recognition test different output transformations. Limited-data comparisons demonstrate the value of the constraints. The transformation is specified for labeled skeletons. Proposal 2 must infer which channel a detector actually placed first at a particular time. [Proceedings and paper](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper/2019/hash/1f88c7c5d7d94ae08bd752aa3d82108b-Abstract.html)

**04.** **Hafner et al., Learning Latent Dynamics for Planning from Pixels (ICML 2019), Fig. 2, §§3–5.** Can action-conditioned predictions support online planning directly from images? PlaNet combines deterministic recurrent memory, stochastic state, learned emissions and rewards, and plans candidate action sequences in latent space. Six continuous-control tasks include comparisons against wholly deterministic or wholly stochastic dynamics and multistep-training variants. Task returns support the use of a recurrent stochastic state under partial observation. The latent coordinates lack a specified physical meaning. Use an equally conditioned recurrent state-space baseline in Proposals 1–3. [Proceedings and paper](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v97/hafner19a.html)

**05.** **van der Pol et al., MDP Homomorphic Networks: Group Symmetries in Reinforcement Learning (NeurIPS 2020), §§2–4, Table 1.** How can a policy exploit symmetric state-action pairs beyond augmenting its experience? The authors connect MDP homomorphisms with tied policy/value maps under a known group preserving transition and reward, and compare appropriate bases to random or complementary bases, ordinary networks and augmentation on CartPole, Pong and a grid world. The results demonstrate faster learning in these tasks. Physical mirroring in all four proposals must transform registered actions consistently. An observational relabeling calls for a different map. [Proceedings and paper](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper/2020/hash/2be5f9c2e3620eb73c2972d7552b6cb5-Abstract.html)

**06.** **Smidt, Geiger & Miller, Finding Symmetry Breaking Order Parameters with Euclidean Neural Networks (Physical Review Research 2021), Eqs. 3–4 and Figs. 1–3.** How can a constrained model diagnose a missing symmetry-breaking input? An equivariant deterministic map must preserve every symmetry of its input. The authors also analyze gradients of invariant losses and optimize additional tensor inputs to reproduce square-to-rectangle deformations and octahedral tilts. These demonstrations show how additional variables can make an output symmetry-compatible. Several physical mechanisms can still have the same symmetry type, the attribution problem in Proposal 3. [Journal article](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevResearch.3.L012002)

**07.** **Kaba & Ravanbakhsh, Symmetry Breaking and Equivariant Neural Networks (NeurReps 2023 extended abstract, revised 2024), Props. 1–3 and Thm. 5.** When does a known distributional symmetry overconstrain a predictor on individual samples? Building on Curie's principle and work on canonicalization, the authors analyze input stabilizers and construct a relaxation in equivariant multilayer perceptrons. The quantitative linear bound on departures near symmetry uses an explicit Lipschitz constant. Examples in physics and graphs illustrate the motivation. This theory guides the ambiguous inputs in Proposals 1 and 2. Relaxation alone supplies no side measurement. [Workshop record](https://openreview.net/forum?id=d55JaRL9wh) · [Revised text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.09016)

**08.** **Lawrence, Portilheiro, Zhang & Kaba, Improving Equivariant Networks with Probabilistic Symmetry Breaking (ICLR 2025), Thm. 3.4 and §§4–8.** How can a model produce an asymmetric realization from an exactly symmetric input while respecting equivariance in distribution? Extending probabilistic symmetry and canonicalization, the authors characterize equivariant conditional laws under group and measurability assumptions and introduce a sampled positional encoding. They test graph reconstruction, molecular graph diffusion and Ising ground states, comparing symmetry-breaking strategies. These findings establish expressive mechanisms for uncertainty. Proposal 2 tests whether posterior probabilities track the reliability of actual side evidence. [Conference paper](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/hash/c7138635035501eb71b0adf6ddc319d6-Abstract-Conference.html) · [Probabilistic foundation, Bloem-Reddy & Teh, JMLR 2020](https://jmlr.org/papers/v21/19-322.html)

## Compression and decision-relevant representations

**09.** **Chen et al., ICML 2020.** Which design choices make two-view contrastive pretraining transfer? SimCLR simplifies instance discrimination, varies augmentation, head, batch and training length, then probes ImageNet and transfer tasks. Section 4.2 and Table 3 probe the same transformations before and after projection. Recoverability depends on the layer. Probe both locations in a pretraining comparison and measure action regret separately. [A Simple Framework for Contrastive Learning of Visual Representations](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v119/chen20j.html)

**10.** **Garrido, Najman and LeCun, ICML 2023.** Can a representation retain object identity and rotation under known transformations? SIE adds separate invariant and equivariant components and a transformation-conditioned hypernetwork to joint-embedding learning. Sections 4–6 and Appendix B ablate the split and predictor on 3DIEBench's controlled object renderings. Retrieval and rotation probes improve in this setting. A fixed split is a comparator for Proposal 1, which asks when a future action justifies storage. [Self-supervised Learning of Split Invariant Equivariant Representations](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v202/garrido23b.html)

**11.** **Garrido et al., arXiv 2024.** Can the predictive head of an image joint-embedding model capture and reuse the effects of augmentations? Image World Models predict transformed image embeddings. Sections 4–6 vary photometric difficulty, conditioning and head capacity, and compare retrieval, probing and predictor fine-tuning. These manipulations change what the predictor retains on ImageNet. Proposal 1 should match decoder capacity and action information. The tested transformations are image operations. [Learning and Leveraging World Models in Visual Representation Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.00504)

**12.** **Gupta et al., NeurIPS 2024.** Can demonstrations specify at inference which transformations matter? ContextSSL conditions a transformer on example state, action and successor triples. A context mask discourages shortcuts and an auxiliary predictor encourages sensitivity. Section 4 compares varying contexts on controlled object views and additional transformations. It demonstrates contextual adaptation when the examples identify the intended symmetry. Count any such context as memory if available at Proposal 1 deployment. [In-Context Symmetries](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/hash/bcad07d4bfab51243efaa08b8ed475b3-Abstract-Conference.html)

**13.** **Ghaemi, Muller and Bakhtiari, NeurIPS 2025.** Could multiple views reconcile single-view transformation sensitivity with stable object identity? seq-JEPA feeds encoded views and known relative actions to an aggregator, then predicts the next embedding. Sections 4–6 and Appendix B assess classification, transformation retrieval, sequence length, saccades and path integration. Its aggregate and single-view representations serve different tasks. Use it as precedent for sequence conditioning. Physical response under a bit budget remains open. [seq-JEPA](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/hash/2f63d2963526bdd9ff1b8bcc2dc9905a-Abstract-Conference.html)

**14.** **Lee et al., ICLR 2026.** Where should a self-supervised image encoder enforce transformation sensitivity? SER uses known geometric actions to align intermediate token maps while preserving the final invariance loss. Section 4 and Appendix A separate regularization from changes in the view distribution, compare matched view counts and test ImageNet and COCO transfer. Results favor intermediate regularization in the tested ViTs. Proposal 1 should compare training at equal numbers of views and audit what persists beyond the regulated layer. [Soft Equivariance Regularization for Invariant Self-Supervised Learning](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2026/hash/3be6511c8f56d0dca4b5ed59fdf9b2f4-Abstract-Conference.html)

**15.** **Arumugam and Van Roy, NeurIPS 2022.** What part of an uncertain environment should an exploring agent learn? Building on posterior sampling and Grimm et al.'s Bellman-update criterion, the authors compress a posterior over finite-horizon episodic MDPs using approximate value equivalence. Sections 4–5 derive Bayesian regret and capacity bounds under stated policy, value-function and information conditions. The information rate concerns the model of the environment. Proposal 1 charges physical bits of history available per episode and needs a decision-trained codec. [Deciding What to Model](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2022/hash/3b18d368150474ac6fc9bb665d3eb3da-Abstract-Conference.html) · [The Value Equivalence Principle](https://proceedings.nips.cc/paper_files/paper/2020/hash/3bb585ea00014b0e3ebe4c6dd165a358-Abstract.html)

**16.** **Banerjee and Montúfar, IJCNN 2020.** How costly is approximating a statistical channel through an intermediate representation? The paper builds on Blackwell's comparison of experiments, optimizes a variational deficiency bound and tests classification. Proposition 3 gives equality between input KL deficiency and the excess Bayes log loss for the specified class of channel predictions. Proposal 1's fixed codec, several interventions and physical loss require their own decision-risk calculation. [The Variational Deficiency Bottleneck](https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.11677)

**17.** **Gonzalez Laiz and Schneider, arXiv 2026.** Do high-return world models predict their own encoded trajectories? Sections 4–6 compare TD-MPC2, DreamerV3 and LeWM on three deterministic control systems, with action-conditioned latent rollouts, state probes, added velocities and invertible or approximately invertible observation changes. Return and rollout consistency can diverge in these settings. Their target includes injectivity of the state representation. Proposal 1 deliberately allows states with identical decision consequences to merge and scores physical loss. [Understanding the Limits of Latent World Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37588)

**18.** **Lawrence et al., ICLR 2026.** Does the empirical input distribution respect a proposed symmetry, and when does bias affect prediction? The authors train a two-sample classifier on original and randomly transformed observations, derive restricted ridge-regression results and compare controlled and real point clouds, including materials data. Sections 2, 4 and 5 distinguish input asymmetry from its task dependence. Use a mirror-discrimination audit to uncover rendering shortcuts, then intervene on those cues to assess physical relevance. [To Augment or Not to Augment?](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2026/hash/106509d2c9263224610fe4145fbed3e8-Abstract-Conference.html)

**19.** **Chadha and Viswanathan, ACM Transactions on Computational Logic 2010.** How can a model checker refine a probabilistic abstraction after an apparent safety violation? Sections 3–5 develop structured counterexamples for finite MDPs, simulation-based validity tests and refinement of abstract partitions against temporal-logic properties. This supplies the search, check and refine precedent. Proposal 1 instead estimates a decision-loss gap conditional on observable history and must beat a generic refinement rule with the same access. [A Counterexample-Guided Abstraction-Refinement Framework for Markov Decision Processes, authors' journal manuscript](https://vmahesh.cs.illinois.edu/papers/tocl10.pdf)

## Temporal memory and side assignment

**20.** **Lan et al., SWAP: Symmetric Equivariant World-Model for Agile Robot Parkour (June 2026 preprint), Fig. 2, Eqs. 3–9, Tables I–II.** Can bilateral sharing in a learned world model and policy improve quadruped parkour? A reflection-equivariant encoder, recurrent state, prior, posterior, decoder and actor-critic act on paired channels. The study compares hard constraints, a loss penalty and ablations on mirrored terrain, with hardware demonstrations. Its registered depth, proprioceptive and action transforms guide the matched reflection-aware baselines. Proposal 1 also quantizes persistent memory, and Proposal 2 adds a detector naming process. Transfer across mirrored terrain does not measure correction of independently swapping detector labels. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.19928)

**21.** **Lillemark et al., Flow Equivariant World Models: Structured Memory for Dynamic Environments (ICML 2026), §§3–5 and Apps. C–H.** How should a visual world model maintain moving objects beyond the current field of view? FloWM transports structured spatial memory with agent motion and latent object velocity, connecting map memories with equivariance under continuous flows. In controlled moving-digit and 3D block scenes, ablations of self-motion transport and velocity channels and comparisons against diffusion and memory models assess long-horizon prediction. A planning study reuses the representation. Direction 1 must isolate the marginal value of storing decision-relevant evidence above structured memory. [Proceedings](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v306/lillemark26a.html) · [Full revised paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.01075v3)

**22.** **Aoyagi, Adachi, Yomo & Miyamoto, SwapPF: Correcting HPE Left-Right Swaps for Gait Analysis Using Particle Filters (ICPRAM 2026), §§3–4, Eq. 13, Table 1.** Can gait periodicity repair side swaps in framewise pose detections? Extending the group's earlier identity-transition particle filter, SwapPF extrapolates leg opening angles from extrema and chooses a channel assignment by squared residual. On IPLabDataset2021, four-fold person identification with the same pose estimates and an SVM reached 87.76% across videos, versus 85.87% unfiltered and 85.98% with the simple filter. Cycle stability and bilateral symmetry are proxy measurements. This directly motivates evaluating genuine asymmetric responses in Proposal 2. The full table supports a 1.89 percentage-point overall improvement over unfiltered poses. [Publisher full paper](https://www.scitepress.org/Papers/2026/144493/144493.pdf)

**23.** **Díaz-Guerra et al., Permutation Invariant Recurrent Neural Networks for Sound Source Tracking Applications (Forum Acusticum 2023), §§2–3, Figs. 5–7.** How can a tracker associate unordered detections with consistent source trajectories? Attention from recurrent tracks to new detections respects input permutations and transports track labels under a state permutation. Building on sliding permutation-invariant training, the authors compare an icosahedral CNN alone, conventional GRUs and the proposed tracker in simulated reverberant rooms with appearing and disappearing speech sources. Reported improvements in tracking support an association baseline for Proposal 2. External anatomical labels require an independent reference. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.08510) · [Earlier sliding-window loss](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.14536)

**24.** **Balsells-Rodas, Wang & Li, On the Identifiability of Switching Dynamical Systems (ICML 2024), Thms. 3.2 and 3.5, §6.** Can observed sequences identify a latent model with discrete regimes and continuous dynamics? Extending finite-mixture and identifiable generative-model results, the authors prove permutation identifiability for specified first-order nonlinear Gaussian regime transitions. Under additional piecewise-linear emission and injectivity conditions they obtain affine equivalence for the latent prior, and under stronger conditions for the emission. Synthetic experiments, climate regime analysis and dance-video segmentation accompany the theorems. These equivalences leave absolute laterality open in Proposal 2. [Proceedings](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v235/balsells-rodas24a.html) · [Mixture-prior antecedent, Kivva et al., NeurIPS 2022](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2022/hash/649f080d8891ab4d4b262cb9cd52e69a-Abstract-Conference.html)

**25.** **Su et al., Leveraging Symmetry in RL-based Legged Locomotion Control (IROS 2024), §§III–VI.** Can morphological symmetry help a controller explore left and right solutions equally? The authors compare PPO, symmetry augmentation and hard policy/value constraints on door pushing, dribbling, stand turning and slope walking, followed by hardware tests. For periodic alternating movement, their reflection advances normalized gait phase by half a cycle modulo one. This is a specified spatiotemporal assumption. Directions 1 and 2 should vary phase separately from persistent gain and test departures from the alternating gait. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.17320) · [Published version, IROS 2024](https://doi.org/10.1109/IROS58592.2024.10802439)

**26.** **Nguyen et al., Equivariant Reinforcement Learning under Partial Observability (CoRL 2023), Thm. 1, §§6.1–6.5.** When does symmetry carry from an environment to a history-conditioned policy? With compatible transition, observation, reward and initial belief, a group-invariant partially observed decision process admits symmetric optimal value structure. A recurrent equivariant actor-critic tests this on hidden-goal grids and robot manipulation requiring memory of prior probes, with sim-to-real evaluation. This is a close precedent for remembering hidden mechanical properties in Proposals 1 and 2. Forecast targets under corrupt names and matched switching filters add distinct tests. [Proceedings and paper](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v229/nguyen23a.html)

## Physical mechanisms and intervention targets

**27.** **Wang, Walters & Yu, ICML 2022. Approximately Equivariant Networks for Imperfectly Symmetric Dynamics.** How much sharing helps when flow dynamics violate the assumed symmetry? Starting from group and steerable convolutions, the authors relax kernel sharing and analyze approximation error under norm-preserving actions. Simulated smoke with controlled violations and measured NASA jet velocities test autoregressive forecasts against strict and unconstrained networks. Relaxation improves several forecasts. Its weights alone cannot say which physical mechanism caused an error. Compare a relaxed predictor on the same intervention records as Proposal 3. [Paper, §§3 and 5, Appendix A](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v162/wang22aa.html)

**28.** **Wang, Hofgard, Gao, Walters & Smidt, ICML 2024. Discovering Symmetry Breaking in Physical Systems with Relaxed Group Convolution.** Can learned relaxation reveal the surviving symmetries within a candidate group? The paper extends relaxed convolutions by interpreting their group-indexed weights. From equivariant initialization the gradient construction preserves common input/output symmetries. The minibatch analysis imposes extra loss assumptions. Crystal transformations, turbulent flows and damped pendulums supply physically interpretable checks. A matched remaining subgroup can still describe several distinct causes. Proposal 3 uses intervention-specific changes to distinguish them. [Paper, Propositions 3.1–3.2, §§5–6](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v235/wang24y.html)

**29.** **Pertigkiozoglou, Petrache, Trivedi & Daniilidis, ICML 2026. Recurrent Equivariant Constraint Modulation.** Previous relaxation schedules require a target level. RECM learns layer-specific mixtures of equivariant and unrestricted maps from training supervision. Its recurrent update samples group transformations and bounds limiting modulation by a distributional symmetry gap under the convergence and regularity hypotheses. Rotated versus aligned point clouds, trajectory prediction and molecular conformers test prediction quality. A layer's relaxation describes its input–target distribution. Two mechanisms that generate that distribution can require identical modulation. [Paper, §4, Lemma 4.1, Theorem 4.2 and §5](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v306/pertigkiozoglou26a.html)

**30.** **Lippe et al., ICML 2022. CITRIS: Causal Identifiability from Temporal Intervened Sequences.** Can temporal order and intervention-target indicators recover multidimensional causal factors from images? Building on variational disentanglement, the authors learn assignments to blocks in a conditional transition prior, including a residual block. For temporally resolved dynamics with distinguishable targets and the stated optimum, identifiability applies to the part of each factor changed by its intervention. Rendered 3D objects and interventional Pong test factor recovery and recombination. The untouched body response in Proposal 3 can reside in residual information. Give the comparator the same repeated-body support and target metadata, then score predicted responses. [Paper, Theorem 3.3, §6 and Appendix D](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v162/lippe22a.html)

**31.** **Li, Kaba & Ravanbakhsh, AISTATS 2025. On the Identifiability of Causal Abstractions.** Brehmer et al. established recovery from pre/post pairs under sufficiently exhaustive single-target interventions. Li et al. allow arbitrary unknown target subsets in a paired design with shared unaffected noise. Under faithfulness and smooth invertible mixing, the non-descendant sets define identifiable groupings, including some that never receive a direct intervention. A small linear Gaussian likelihood experiment illustrates the result. Proposal 3 needs a registered physical response estimand and dynamical design, since indirect identification alone is already covered. [Paper, Theorems 3.1–3.2 and Appendix D](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v258/li25g.html) · [Brehmer et al., NeurIPS 2022](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2022/hash/fa567e2b2c870f8f09a87b6e73370869-Abstract-Conference.html)

**32.** **Varıcı, Acartürk, Shanmugam & Tajer, AISTATS 2024. General Identifiability and Achievability for Causal Representation Learning.** Can a constructive method recover latent variables through a general nonlinear observation map when intervention environments lack pairing metadata? GSCALE-I analyzes how gradients of log densities vary across environments. The main result uses observational data and two sufficiently distinct hard interventions per node, with recovery up to relabeling and elementwise transforms. The synthetic five- and eight-node experiments use the easier coupled setting. Proposal 3 studies a body that never receives those interventions and measures a calibrated response in physical units. [Paper, Theorems 1–3 and §6](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v238/varici24a.html)

**33.** **Saengkyongam, Rosenfeld, Ravikumar, Pfister & Peters, ICLR 2024. Identifying Representations for Intervention Extrapolation.** When can a learned representation predict an intervention outside training support? Rep4Ex-CF combines a conditional moment restriction with the econometric control-function approach. Exogenous actions act linearly on latent features with full-support residuals. Identifying the representation up to an affine change additionally uses regularity of mixing, noise and action support. Synthetic studies test nonlinear outcome extrapolation. Proposal 3 must state a response law or collect neutral calibration data before extrapolating from reversal settings. [Paper, Proposition 1, Theorems 3 and 5, §6](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/hash/aa9508b2577e0d9c824da9e2e27fe9d6-Abstract-Conference.html)

**34.** **Tolle, Balsells-Rodas, Wang, Mediano & Nejatbakhsh, arXiv v1, 5 November 2026. Identifying Neural Dynamics Using Nonlinear Interventional State Space Models.** Extending an earlier iSSM with linear dynamics, the authors ask which latent coordinate changes survive nonlinear transitions. A smooth injective emission, nondegenerate Gaussian transitions and local full-rank drift give affine equivalence. Sufficiently separating hard intervention masks further restrict it to permutation, scaling and shift with the specified regime support. Tests include simulated excitable systems, macaque recordings and human stimulation in one patient. The authors explicitly place unseen-site extrapolation beyond their guarantee. Body persistence and camera reversal supply different information. [Preprint, Theorems 2–3, Corollary 4 and §5](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08113v1) · [Nejatbakhsh & Wang, ICML 2025](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v267/nejatbakhsh25a.html)

## Adaptive experiments and sequential evidence

**35.** **Foster, Ivanova, Malik and Rainforth, ICML 2021. Deep Adaptive Design.** Can an experimenter plan several informative measurements without expensive posterior optimization between them? Extending Bayesian optimal design, the authors train a history-conditioned policy using contrastive bounds on total information. Their explicit-likelihood implementation uses conditionally independent experimental outcomes given parameters and designs, with continuous designs. Source localization, delay discounting and a death process test information yield and deployment time. The two-step example in §7 shows why greedy information can waste a later observation. Proposal 4 therefore matches lookahead as well as latency. [Paper, Theorems 1–3 and §§6–7](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v139/foster21a.html) · [Full text with appendices](https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.02438)

**36.** **Nick W. Koning, Annals of Statistics 2026. Measuring Evidence against Exchangeability and Group Invariance with E-values.** Can a test statistic quantify evidence against invariance under a compact group and support optional stopping? The paper characterizes orbit-normalized e-values, log-optimal choices and processes for specified filtrations. Section 7 learns a Gaussian alternative from earlier case-control batches and also studies sign flipping and basketball shot sequences. Both the normalization and learning a score on past data predate Proposal 4. The proposal must justify conditional invariance for each *chosen* experiment and demonstrate efficient design of the next one. [Journal record](https://doi.org/10.1214/26-AOS2627) · [Full text, Corollary 3 and §§5–7](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.01153)

**37.** **Grünwald, de Heide and Koolen, JRSS B 2024. Safe Testing.** How can evidence remain valid when the next study depends on earlier results? Starting with nonnegative variables of null expectation at most one, the authors derive safe combination rules and optimize expected logarithmic growth, including composite problems and nuisance parameters. Their worked examples include the t-test and two-by-two contingency tables. Section 6 examines the relationship between growth and power. Proposal 4 can borrow the conditional betting argument. Maximizing local growth alone supplies no deadline-power guarantee. [Full manuscript, §§3–7, JRSS B 86(5), 1091–1128](https://arxiv.org/abs/1906.07801)

**38.** **Kilian, Cortinovis and Caron, NeurIPS 2025. Anytime-valid, Bayes-assisted, Prediction-Powered Inference.** Can predictions on unlabeled examples shorten sequential intervals for a population quantity? The authors extend prediction-powered inference using control variates, mixture boundaries and strong approximation assumptions. They study noisy and biased predictions and replay labeled and unlabeled streams from datasets including flights, forests and galaxies. Their general guarantee is asymptotic and time-uniform. Proposal 4 instead has a finite-sample conditional symmetry null. The common experimental question is how much useful precision or power a possibly wrong model buys. [Conference paper, Theorems 3–5 and §§5–6](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/hash/01830c92c6558179fa6d7fb1edff692c-Abstract-Conference.html) · [Full text with supplement](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.18000)

**39.** **Nitinawarat, Atia and Veeravalli, IEEE Transactions on Automatic Control 2013. Controlled Sensing for Multihypothesis Testing.** When does choosing the next sensor improve discrimination? Extending Chernoff's sequential design, the paper studies known simple hypotheses with observation laws conditional on the selected action. For binary fixed-horizon testing a fixed pure control attains the optimal asymptotic error exponent. A three-hypothesis example exhibits an adaptive advantage. Modified Chernoff rules achieve first-order optimality in the sequential setting under the stated discrimination conditions. Proposal 4 needs fixed-probe and fitted controlled-sensing baselines and explicit finite-budget evaluation. [Full paper, Proposition 1, Theorems 2–4 and §V](https://arxiv.org/abs/1205.0858) · [Chernoff, 1959](https://doi.org/10.1214/aoms/1177706205)

**40.** **Nitinawarat and Veeravalli, Sequential Analysis 2015. Controlled Sensing for Sequential Multihypothesis Testing with Controlled Markovian Observations and Non-Uniform Control Cost.** Does cost-efficient discrimination survive observation memory? The paper extends the memoryless theory using finite, positive controlled Markov kernels, exploration and a self-tuning likelihood rule. Theorems 4.1 and 5.1 give matching first-order bounds on delay or cost as error vanishes. The discussion identifies incomplete knowledge of the response laws as an open limitation of the construction. For Proposal 4, unequal costs and adaptivity alone are established territory. Compare both known and fitted models at a finite cap. [Full paper and proof, journal DOI 10.1080/07474946.2014.961864](https://arxiv.org/abs/1310.1844)

## Interpreting empirical controls

**41.** **Lin et al., CVPR 2020.** How can a classifier distinguish a photograph from its reflection? The authors combine original-versus-flipped classification and cue localization with a commutation analysis and controlled noise-image tests of Bayer demosaicing, JPEG compression and cropping. Sections 3–6 isolate both low-level and semantic cues. A renderer can provide a reliable orientation cue even when body gains are equal. Randomize image processing independently of physical side. [Visual Chirality](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_CVPR_2020/papers/Lin_Visual_Chirality_CVPR_2020_paper.pdf)

**42.** **Achille and Soatto, JMLR 2018.** When does information minimality imply invariance to nuisance variables? Proposition 3.1 and its corollaries connect task-sufficient representations and dependence on a nuisance under an information bottleneck and a Markov structure. The authors also study noisy weights and test invariance under controlled synthetic nuisance transformations. In Proposal 1 the downstream action and loss determine which variation is expendable. A gait phase that changes the preferred pulse can merit a bit. [Emergence of Invariance and Disentanglement in Deep Representations](https://jmlr.org/papers/v19/17-646.html)

**43.** **Guo et al., ICML 2017.** Do modern classifiers' confidences match empirical accuracy, and can scaling repair them? They evaluate reliability diagrams, bin-based calibration error and held-out temperature scaling across image and text classifiers. Sections 3–5 show a practical scalar correction often works on the evaluated distributions. For Proposal 2 check frequency reliability across swap rates and bodies as well as symmetry consistency. Marginal confidence calibration supplies no guarantee for a shifted conditional posterior. [On Calibration of Modern Neural Networks](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v70/guo17a.html)

**44.** **Chen et al., ICML 2018.** Can shared-trunk multitask training adjust task weights online? GradNorm controls gradient norms using relative task training rates, with an exponent controlling asymmetry. Sections 3–5 compare against fixed weights, grid search and uncertainty weighting in synthetic multitask regression and visual prediction. If Proposal 1 uses auxiliary sign labels, compare tuned weighting with this adaptive control and give every supervised comparator the same labels. Balanced norms alone do not equate tasks. [GradNorm](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v80/chen18a.html)

## Additional comparisons that changed the proposals

- **Tests of future actions.** Predictive state representations already describe state through predictions of prescribed action-observation tests. In finite linear settings, a core test set can span other predictions. A small intervention bank can therefore be an old idea in a new application. Proposal 1 must show that reflection reduces the search or memory required for a *decision*, and include a loss-trained codec. [Littman, Sutton and Singh, *Predictive Representations of State*, NeurIPS 2001](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2001/file/1e4d36177d71bbb3558e43af9577d70e-Paper.pdf)
- **Conditional filtering.** Rao-Blackwellized particle filters sample some state components and integrate tractable conditional components. This is the natural comparator for summing over two naming assignments. Proposal 2 varies reflection sharing independently of whether a filter commits to one assignment. [Doucet et al., *Rao-Blackwellised Particle Filtering for Dynamic Bayesian Networks*, UAI 2000](https://arxiv.org/abs/1301.3853)
- **Reversal, allocation and context adaptation.** Precision metrology separates certain errors by reversing an instrument. Targeted optimal design already allows correlated records, and CoDA adapts dynamics using context parameters. Proposal 3 compares the physical contrast and learned predictor under the same allocation of independent runs and repeated views. This tests whether a learned representation saves physical trials beyond a calibrated design. [Flack and Hannaford, National Physical Laboratory, pp. 87–90](https://www.npl.co.uk/special-pages/gpg80_metrology#page=103) · [Holland-Letz, Dette and Pepelyshev, JRSS B 2011](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3106301/) · [Kirchmeyer et al., *CoDA*, ICML 2022](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v162/kirchmeyer22a.html)
- **Active testing without a known model.** Hsu and Shekhar choose between sources, receive independent two-sample pairs from the selected source, and use nonparametric betting. Under their conditions they control false rejection and bound stopping time. Li et al. actively query costly labels for observed features to test equality of distributions. Proposal 4 instead chooses a physical pulse before observing a trajectory. Its paired-probe arm can compare directly with multi-source betting, with both physical executions charged. [Hsu and Shekhar, ISIT 2026](https://doi.org/10.1109/ISIT62367.2026.11654164) · [Full manuscript, Theorem 1 and Corollary 1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.22403) · [Li et al., TMLR 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.12616)
