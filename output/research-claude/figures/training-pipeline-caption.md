# Figure 1: training and evaluation pipeline

**File:** [training-pipeline.svg](training-pipeline.svg) (editable vector source) · [training-pipeline.png](training-pipeline.png) (2× raster preview, rendered with headless Chrome)

**Caption.** The pipeline from simulation to a locked test. Whole body families are split before any episode, window or mirror copy exists. Each stage keeps the time grid, units and left/right channel names; the strip under each stage states what it preserves and how it keeps test information out. The test split is scored once, against the exact Bayes predictor.

**Alt text.** Five cards from left to right, joined by arrows. Card 1, "Sample families", shows two carts seen from above, each with a left and a right motor; in the first the left thrust arrow is longer (b greater than zero, left stronger), in the second the right one is (minus b, right stronger), separated by a dashed mirror line. Card 2, "Split families first", shows three bars labeled training, validation and locked test, the last with a padlock. Card 3, "Probe, hold, query", shows a timeline in three shaded bands: the differential command pulses during the probe, is flat during the hold and steps up for the query, while total force wiggles during the probe and is flat during the hold, labeled "same for b and minus b"; array shapes for observations, commands and targets are listed below. Card 4, "Signed-belief model", shows an encoder feeding a state split into even and odd halves; the odd half feeds a two-bar belief for left and right, which is multiplied with the command u before a decoder produces the response. Card 5, "Score once", shows a scatter plot of model predictions against the Bayes mean of the contrast, with a dashed identity line for the Bayes reference and a shallower solid line for a model that shrinks the asymmetry, and two chips defining contrast skill S (1 equals the Bayes predictor, 0 equals a symmetric body). Under every card a grey strip lists what the stage keeps and its leakage guard. A bracket beneath cards 2 to 4 notes that training reads only the training split, validation chooses settings, and the test split stays sealed until stage 5. A key at the bottom defines even (blue) and odd (coral) quantities, the dashed Bayes line and the solid model line, and states that the mirror swaps left and right and flips odd quantities, so that an odd-times-odd product such as b times u can change total force.

**Design notes.** One idea per card, a five-step left-to-right reading order, and a single consistent color code: blue for even quantities, coral for odd ones, teal, indigo and amber for the three splits.
