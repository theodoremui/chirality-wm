# Review protocol and provenance

The requested `codex:adversarial-review` capability was absent from the exposed tools, MCP resources, and searched local skills/plugins. Independent Codex reviewer agents supply the adversarial reviews. This package does not claim invocation of that unavailable capability or use of Claude; directory names follow the user’s request.

Each proposal is written before its review. The next version incorporates selected feedback; decisions are recorded in `revision-ledger.md`. Earlier versions remain intact. Version 7 receives final corrections and re-review before sealing. These are seven iterations of one research program.

Scores assess **proposal design**, conditional on future experiments. They are neither acceptance probabilities nor empirical evidence. Scale: 0 absent/incorrect, 1 seriously deficient, 2 weak, 3 credible with gaps, 4 strong, 5 unusually complete; half-points allowed. Total = sum of weight × score / 5.

| Dimension | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Significance | 12 |
| Novelty | 18 |
| Conceptual and mathematical correctness | 12 |
| Method specificity | 12 |
| Identification and causal validity | 10 |
| Data fidelity and leakage control | 10 |
| Experiments and baselines | 10 |
| Statistics and falsifiability | 8 |
| Feasibility | 4 |
| Clarity | 4 |

Empirical readiness is separate: no new neural training, benchmark acquisition, or human analysis is completed here. Analytical checks and document validation cannot replace those experiments. The source auditor’s original grade uses its separately disclosed rubric; a common-rubric original grade will be included in the scorecard for longitudinal comparisons.
