# Training-pipeline figure

**Caption.** Training a predictive world model to retain signed bilateral mechanics in the controlled cart benchmark. Immutable episodes retain actuator side labels, timestamps, and recorded commands. Families are assigned to training, validation, or locked testing before constructing causal windows; all mirrored copies and intervention branches remain in the same partition. Observations have shape `[B,T,3]`, with columns position `x`, velocity `v`, and total force `F_total`; controls have shape `[B,T,2]`, with common input `a` and differential input `u`. Masks preserve missing or padded slots. A diagnostic interaction provides evidence about mechanics, a delay tests its retention, and a known query schedule tests whether the retained evidence improves physical-response forecasts. The candidate updates changing state from real observations, retains context after diagnosis, and holds a sampled context fixed within each forecast rollout. Every predictive sample produces a complete physical trajectory; integrating its interval-average force yields impulse, with no separate impulse head. Training uses a composite trajectory score (including the ordinary history and query-trajectory terms) and impulse CRPS (continuous ranked probability score), which evaluates the predictive distribution while penalizing inaccurate location and unnecessary spread. These dependent targets do not form a likelihood of independent observations. Validation selects the checkpoint. Locked testing freezes model weights while allowing protocol-defined state filtering from real observations, then measures response error, calibration, and memory with independent families as the evaluation units. The figure shows a proposed procedure, not completed experiments.

**Alt text.** A six-stage flow begins with immutable actuator episodes, splits complete families before windowing, and constructs causal observation tensors `[B,T,3]` and control tensors `[B,T,2]` with masks. A diagnostic interaction is followed by a delay and a future query. A recurrent belief updates state from real data and retains diagnostic context, which is held fixed within each forecast rollout. It predicts a trajectory distribution and integrates force to obtain impulse. A green training lane feeds measured branch targets into a trajectory score plus impulse CRPS and updates model weights. A separate gray test lane sends held-out bodies and schedules through a model with frozen weights and continued state filtering, then assesses response error, calibration, and memory. Test targets never feed the training path.

**Interpretation notes.** The figure follows [the final proposal](../../../notes/archive/ideas-codex/v07_predictive_memory_for_asymmetric_mechanics.md). “Exact shape” refers to preserving raw physical values, side indexing, and observed temporal order; the documented normalized view is invertible, whereas a learned latent compression need not be. Fixed-length batching may add padding, which is explicitly masked. The gated candidate accumulates context evidence during diagnosis and copies it afterward; the comparator with unrestricted context updates can use later real observations. Both hold each sampled persistent context fixed during a forecast rollout. The exact diagnostic makes neutral post-reset observations uninformative about imbalance; in the nonlinear condition these observations can refine nuisance uncertainty and thereby change an all-observations posterior over imbalance. Continued state filtering is distinct from updating trained weights or feeding forecast samples back as observations. A locked-test memory probe uses a readout fitted on training families and selected on validation families; its fitting must not touch test labels. A mirror transformation applies to all physical quantities and controls together and never moves an episode across partitions. A future skeleton extension would separately declare a joint dimension `J` and its anatomical permutation; no such dimension is implied for the cart observation tensor.
