# Literature and novelty audit

Audited 23 October 2026. This audit checks primary publication records, selected full text, and author project pages. It verifies that sources exist and what they claim; it does not independently reproduce their experiments. Recent preprints remain preprints unless a primary record reports acceptance. The supplied proposal's six recent machine-learning references resolve to the stated works. None should be called fabricated because its date is recent.

## The most defensible research gap

The proposal should study whether a learned predictive state retains **persistent, signed mechanical information after the observations that identified it have left the immediate input window**, and whether retaining that information improves response forecasts for new commands. The distinctive evaluation would compare models with the same observations, calibration interactions, training targets, memory budget, and inference-time access. This is a narrower and more testable claim than inventing equivariant recurrence, online system identification, or action-conditioned JEPA.

The original proposal already separates measurement ambiguity from representation loss. Make this separation the organizing experiment: an unidentifiable passive condition; an identifiable calibration condition; a delay during which evidence must be retained; and a novel command whose response depends on the signed mechanics. A failure against an ordinary estimator on the same records is meaningful. A failure caused by giving the estimator additional sensors or simulator parameters is not.

## Decisions recommended for the seven revisions

1. **Retain the analytical example and simplify the surrounding story.** It is useful because it fixes which sensor and input expose the signed gain. Present it as a sanity check, not a novel theorem about human gait.
2. **Treat ordinary identification as a serious competitor.** Include a raw-history estimator plus forecast model, a probabilistic context-inference model, a generic RSSM, and an equivariant RSSM. Match data access and tuning budget. An invariant model is a diagnostic negative control, not the strongest baseline.
3. **Put any new loss on trial.** Outcome pairs transformed into sums, differences, or derivatives do not create new data. A candidate retention loss should survive comparisons with longer training horizons, balanced sampling of informative transitions, and equivalent loss rescaling.
4. **Separate structural bias from information preservation.** Equivariance permits a signed state; it does not ensure that optimization uses it. An explicit odd channel also admits the zero solution. Crossed ablations should isolate recurrence, parity structure, auxiliary supervision, and uncertainty.
5. **Make forecasting the endpoint.** Decoding a parameter sign from an embedding is supportive. Better forecasts on held-out command schedules after a retention delay are stronger evidence of usable information. Include proper distributional scores and ambiguity controls.
6. **Bound the theory.** A transparent non-identifiability argument under a specified sensor/probe family is valuable. Do not infer human causal identification from synthetic paired branches or from posterior confidence.
7. **Keep active experiment design optional.** A fixed, matched probing protocol is enough to establish a retention gap. Active probing creates another difficult contribution and should enter only after the core comparison works.

## Chemistry assertion and unresolved evidence

The original proposal's statement about Henri Kagan and Kenso Soai receiving the 2026 Nobel Prize in Chemistry is corroborated by the [French Académie des sciences announcement dated 7 October 2026](https://www.academie-sciences.fr/en/henri-kagan-member-academy-sciences-and-kenso-soai-winners-2026-nobel-prize-chemistry). Direct fetches of the Nobel press-release and summary pages returned HTTP 403 during this audit. That is an access limitation, not evidence that the claim is false. For an ICML proposal, remove the prize framing because it does little technical work and consumes space needed for the learning contribution. The chemistry analogy is optional historical motivation; it cannot justify a neural training objective or claims about gait mechanisms.

The supplied manuscripts and source proposal remain local, unpublished evidence unless their publication status is independently established. The original proposal names a local `ICLR_2027.pdf`; this audit does not assume that it is identical to the supplied `jepa_asymmetry_paper.pdf`. Human dataset sizes, calibration, laterality, synchronization, and access conditions require a separate archive-level audit. Citation verification alone does not validate those operational claims.

## Evidence boundary

The recommendations above are research judgments drawn from the verified sources. No literature search establishes novelty by absence. Before submission, rerun forward/backward citation search around SWAP, ConDA, embodiment adaptation, predictive-state sufficiency, and equivariant system identification, then compare the final implemented method and experiments—not only its title—to the closest available work.

## Replication resources checked during the final revisions

The official [dm_control walker model](https://github.com/google-deepmind/dm_control/blob/main/dm_control/suite/walker.xml) defines three root coordinates and six bilateral leg joints, with six motors. Its [task implementation](https://github.com/google-deepmind/dm_control/blob/main/dm_control/suite/walker.py) uses a 0.025-second control interval. The proposed replication's position/velocity/foot-force arrays are custom instrumentation, not the environment's stock observation dictionary. Foot forces require verified contact extraction and transformation to the world frame. Preserve nominal actuator settings and apply dimensionless gain multipliers; pin a release or commit rather than relying on the mutable `main` links. These checks verify the planned schema's plausibility, not a completed simulation or a stable open-loop walking controller.

The official [MuJoCo state documentation](https://mujoco.readthedocs.io/en/stable/computation/index.html#the-state) supports saving more than positions and velocities for branch reproduction. Application-side controller and random state must also be preserved when used. The source code and documentation were inspected, but the contact benchmark was not implemented in this task.

Tilmann Gneiting and Adrian E. Raftery, **Strictly Proper Scoring Rules, Prediction, and Estimation**, JASA 102(477), 2007, provides the statistical foundation for CRPS and the energy score. The [author-hosted paper](https://sites.stat.washington.edu/people/raftery/Research/PDF/Gneiting2007jasa.pdf) and [University of Washington record](https://stat.uw.edu/research/tech-reports/strictly-proper-scoring-rules-prediction-and-estimation) were verified. These scores assess predictive distributions without requiring a tractable density. Their expected propriety does not guarantee that a fitted finite-sample model is calibrated; calibration and coverage checks remain necessary.
