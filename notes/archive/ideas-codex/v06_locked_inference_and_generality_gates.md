# Mechanical Memory: A Falsifiable Study with a Locked Evaluation

**Version 6 · Research proposal · 23 October 2026**  
**Status:** proposed experiments, revised after the [version 5 review](../../output/research-codex/review-v05.md).

## Abstract

Predicting a new physical input can require information that was visible only earlier in a trajectory. We propose a bilateral actuator benchmark that makes this requirement testable without confusing it with missing sensors, uncertain nuisance parameters, or train–test overlap. A controlled diagnostic condition has a known observation law: an early response reveals mechanical imbalance, while later neutral measurements carry no further information about it. A nonlinear condition then relaxes the known-parameter assumption and permits later observations to remain useful. Recurrent models, stored diagnostic summaries, and ordinary identification receive the same causal records. A modest persistent-context candidate tests whether an explicit update rule helps under limited memory. The study measures complete force trajectories and their integrated response, while treating any gain over conventional methods as an empirical question.

## Motivation and measurable objective

World models predict how observations change under inputs. Bilateral mechanics provide a simple test of what their internal state must retain: similar total motion can arise from a stronger left actuator or a stronger right actuator, yet the same later differential command can produce different responses. Asymmetry is useful here because it creates a signed physical distinction with a declared transformation law.

The supplied JEPA paper studies pose restoration and reports unresolved incremental benefits. The URTC draft reports complete source-video overlap in its classifier split. Neither provides a completed mechanics-memory result. We retain their lessons about anatomical indexing, trivial controls, and independent experimental units, as documented in the [source audit](../../output/research-codex/source-evidence-audit.md).

**Within twelve weeks, can a persistent-context model reduce nonlinear delayed-impulse MSE by a confidence-supported 5% against the strongest validation-selected recurrent or stored-summary comparator, while meeting near-term and uncertainty guardrails?** The fixed horizon is 0.5 seconds and the primary delay is eight seconds. The exact diagnostic validates the interpretation; the nonlinear condition is the main empirical comparison. All targets, schedules, memory budgets, and tuning access are shared. A null result remains evidence about when conventional identification or simple memory is sufficient.

## Physical systems and limits of the observation claim

A cart obeys $m \ddot x = F_L + F_R - kx - c\dot x$. The actuator equations are $\tau  \dot q_L=\phi (a+u)-q_L$ and $\tau  \dot q_R=\phi (a-u)-q_R$, with forces $(g+b)q_L$ and $(g-b)q_R$. Common input $a=0.6$ is even under reflection, differential input $u$ and imbalance $b$ are odd. Forward position, velocity, and total force are even. Reflection swaps actuator labels; it does not reverse the cart's forward axis.

The **exact diagnostic** fixes and discloses $g=1\,\mathrm{N}$, $\tau =0.1\,\mathrm{s}$, $m=1 \mathrm{kg}$, $k=2 \mathrm{N/m}$, $c=0.4 \mathrm{N\,s/m}$, and $\phi (v)=v$. Only signed $b$ is unknown. Start diagnosis at $x=v=0, q_L=q_R=a$. Following the diagnostic pulse block, set $x=v=0$ and $q_L=q_R=a$, retaining mechanics and model memory. Under subsequent $u=0$, every observation distribution is independent of $b$. Known nuisance values prevent neutral data from refining a nuisance posterior correlated with imbalance. The later impulse under constant query $u$ is

$$
J = 2gaH + 2bu[H - \tau (1 - \exp (-H/\tau ))]
$$


The **nonlinear condition** uses $\phi_\alpha(v)=\frac{\tanh(\alpha v)}{\tanh(\alpha)}$ with unknown $g,\tau ,\alpha ,m$. Initialize diagnosis and reset activations at $\phi _\alpha (a)$, with $x=v=0$ at both boundaries. At fixed nuisance parameters neutral observations are sign-independent, but they can refine nuisance uncertainty and thereby alter the posterior over imbalance. We make no claim that this delay is wholly uninformative. An all-observations Bayesian reference tests that distinction. The gated candidate may discard useful information and is explicitly compared with unrestricted context updating. A no-reset condition measures how much ordinary continuous observations reduce the need for stored diagnosis.

For two equally likely histories with the same stored state and conditional means $m_+$ and $m_-$ under the same future query, the best shared prediction has excess MSE $\frac{(m_+-m_-)^2}{4}$. This elementary diagnostic identity concerns fixed queries, not jointly mirrored body/query pairs. It is not a theorem that trained world models collapse.

## Reproducible episode and split contract

Records use SI units and a 100 Hz observation clock. A two-second diagnosis contains eight 0.25-second blocks: $[+0.10,0,-0.10,0,+0.25,0,-0.25,0]$ for $u$; reversing all pulse signs is sampled independently of the body sign. Delay is selected from 0, 1, 4, or 8 seconds. Query input is drawn from $\{-0.25,-0.10,+0.10,+0.25\}$ and announced only at forecast origin. The simulator uses an internal step of 0.001 seconds, with step-halving checks before data lock.

Nonlinear body support is $g\in [0.8,1.2]\,\mathrm{N}$, $\lvert b\rvert\in [0.1,0.3]\,\mathrm{N}$, $\tau \in [0.05,0.15]\,\mathrm{s}$, $m\in [0.8,1.2] \mathrm{kg}$, and $\alpha \in [0.5,2]$; $k,c$ remain as above. Each family contains both signs of one magnitude draw and all branches, resets, initial-state continuations, mirrors, and noise replicas. No sign pair becomes two independent bodies. Position, velocity, and total-force observation noise have standard deviations 0.001 m, 0.01 m/s, and 0.03 N, respectively. Additional noise settings are sensitivity analyses.

Use 200 separate pilot families, 4,200 training families, 900 validation families, and a pilot-sized confirmation cohort of 200–2,000 families. Assign identifiers before derived windows. Each family has a linear companion with the same imbalance and a nonlinear companion with its sampled nuisances; all models train on an equal mixture of both. A public even regime flag distinguishes the known-nuisance diagnostic from the unknown-nuisance condition and reveals no hidden gain. Both companions remain in one split. Thus the 56-fit registry below covers both regimes, and neither is mislabeled as an in-distribution test of a model trained only on the other. For support-shift testing, reserve the combination $\lvert b\rvert>0.25\,\mathrm{N}$ and $\tau >0.125\,\mathrm{s}$; neither its training nor validation families are admitted. Each marginal range remains represented outside that corner. Independent interpolation confirmation draws from the remaining support. Query extrapolation to magnitude 0.35 is a separate secondary test; inputs remain physically admissible. Save generator version, seeds, parameter support, family membership, and content hashes in a manifest. The pilot uses separate families and freezes the final counts and manifest before confirmation.

Raw observations are `[episode,time,3]` in the order $(x,v,F_{\mathrm{total}})$. Commands are `[episode,time,2]` in the order $(a,u)$. Flags, timestamps, and masks share the time axis, including the even regime indicator. At time $t_k$, observe $y_k$, then apply command $a_k$ on $[t_k,t_{k+1})$. History contains $y_0\ldots y_k$ and previously applied commands, while the announced query is a separate future input. Filters use only that history. A physical reset is recorded as a discontinuity and flag, not silently interpolated.

For each of 50 forecast intervals, store $(x_{\mathrm{end}},v_{\mathrm{end}},F_{\mathrm{average}})$ where the force average is the simulator integral over that interval divided by 0.01 seconds. Target shape is `[batch,50,3]`, and $J=0.01 \sum  F_{\mathrm{average}}$; this removes endpoint-sampling ambiguity. Raw force observations are instantaneous readings and remain separate from these derived training targets. No target enters the query-origin encoder. Padding retains slots and masks; time is never stretched. Exact geometry is not claimed unchanged by normalization: raw values remain immutable and the documented model view is invertible.

Normalization must commute with reflection. Even scalar channels may use training means; odd channels use zero centering and a shared positive training scale. Paired channels, when exposed in an oracle check, use identical scales. Masks and flags are even, and odd state coordinates negate. Check reflection twice returns the original record, transformed predictions obey the declared law, and preprocessing preserves that equality. Branches clone the full physical and random state and pass identical-input replay before use.

## Candidate and matched learning

Store 64 changing-state and 64 context-evidence values, each with even/odd partitions. A shared base update evaluated on an input and its reflection gives parity-consistent half-sum/half-difference updates. Changing state uses every real observation; context accumulates during diagnosis and is copied later. Every model receives the gate. It is justified by the exact diagnostic and is an approximation in the nonlinear condition, where unrestricted updates may help.

For even/odd evidence $(e,o)$, use $P(S=s)\propto \exp  d(e,s o)$, $\log  R\mid S=s\sim \mathcal{N}(\mu (e,s o),\sigma ^2(e,s o))$, and odd context $sR$. Reflection exchanges signs and preserves magnitude laws. Even nuisance context can depend on the same component. Draw context once per query, initialize changing state conditionally, and retain context throughout prediction. Permit odd-by-odd products in even force outputs. The mixture can preserve uncertainty over opposite mechanics, but neither its names nor its architecture guarantee physical identification.

Each sample predicts complete 50-row trajectories and obtains impulse by integration. Train with trajectory energy score plus impulse CRPS, both proper distributional scores in expectation. CRPS rewards distributions that are accurate without unnecessary spread. Fixed scales are $(1\,\mathrm{m},1 \mathrm{m/s},1\,\mathrm{N})$ for trajectory channels and $1 \mathrm{N\,s}$ for impulse; divide trajectory distance by $\sqrt{150}$. Add the common one-step history score; all three coefficients are one. This composite reweights a derived outcome rather than inventing new information.

Use two independent training banks, each with four continuous draws per sign, weighted by mixture probabilities. The cross-bank pair-distance term gives a differentiable score estimator. Evaluation uses two banks of 128 samples each, with 64 per sign; their weighted mean is the MSE point prediction. Check on validation that increasing to 512 per bank changes aggregate risks by less than 10% of the planned improvement margin and never changes selected comparator; otherwise increase the locked sample count for every method and recheck resources. Backpropagate through the full diagnosis and delay. Future query targets enter losses only, with no future-observation teacher forcing.

Compare C against B1 ordinary 128-state recurrence; B2 generic 64+64 context with continuous updates; B3 parity context with continuous updates; B4 generic gated context; B5 a stored 128-value final diagnostic summary; and B6 a 64-value summary plus 64 changing values. All have the same mixture, current origin observation, targets, and decoder inputs. Match useful parameter counts within 10%, reporting actual differences. The constraint is 128 stored float32 values, while transient rollout storage, parity's second evaluation, and total runtime are reported separately. Fixed storage does not imply fixed compute.

Select the primary B by validation nonlinear impulse MSE, with ID-order tie breaking. Full-history learning, recent-window learning, equation-aware identification, fitted-parameter summaries, and privileged mechanics oracles are separate references. They reveal whether a special memory is needed, with their extra storage or model-law knowledge disclosed.

## Evidence for retention

Fit locked linear and two-layer nonlinear readouts to frozen memories at diagnosis end and 1/4/8-second delays. Train each shared readout across these times using training families; tune only on validation. Inputs are memory and query; targets are the same canonical-reset impulse at every delay. This controls changing physical state. Compare paired recoverability deterioration with full-history, recent-window, and no-diagnosis references. A primary forecast gain alone does not establish a memory mechanism, and failed probes establish only failure within tested readout classes.

Secondary forecast queries switch from $+0.25$ to $-0.25$ halfway through the horizon or reverse that order. These are withheld forecast schedules, although diagnostic histories already contain switches. They are never used for tuning. Test amplitude extrapolation and the withheld parameter corner separately; none is silently pooled into the primary result.

## Locked inference and failure accounting

For each confirmation family, average squared error of the predictive mean over four constant queries, both body signs, and four independent sensor-noise histories, then over final training seeds 17, 29, 43, 61, and 89. Repeat this aggregation for CRPS. The primary nonlinear delay is eight seconds; the near-term guardrail is the same half-second query immediately after reset, at zero delay. It is distinct from one-step training loss.

Use 10,000 paired family-bootstrap draws with analysis seed 20261023. Resample whole families, retaining all methods, signs, queries, noises, and seeds together. In each draw recompute risks and nonlinear quantities such as square roots. The one-sided 95% upper percentile bound must satisfy all three conditions: $R_C-0.95R_B<0$, $\operatorname{RMSE}_{C,0}-\operatorname{RMSE}_{B,0}<0.02\,\mathrm{N\,s}$, and $\operatorname{CRPS}_C-\operatorname{CRPS}_B<0.01\,\mathrm{N\,s}$. This intersection-union rule supports one combined improvement claim; it does not confer confirmatory status on individual exploratory contrasts. B is validation-selected and may not be the best model on the test set. The interval conditions on these five fitted seeds; report each seed's effect and avoid a universal training-run claim.

Pilot risk sets $\delta =0.05R_B$, precision $\epsilon =\delta /4$, and approximate size $\left\lceil\left(\frac{1.645s_D}{\epsilon}\right)^2\right\rceil$, capped at 200–2,000 families. Use the larger requirement implied by primary and guardrail precision planning. This approximation is checked by pilot resampling and is not a power guarantee. Insufficient precision at the cap or validation comparator RMSE below 0.01 N·s triggers a limited-evidence or floor conclusion, without relaxing criteria.

Invalid simulator branches are identified by model-independent numerical/physical checks before fitting; reject the whole family and record the reason, using a fixed spare-family stream. Falls in the contact extension are physical outcomes and remain included. A crashed final fit, nonfinite prediction, or scoring failure is reported and vetoes the combined superiority claim until resolved by a documented protocol amendment and a fresh confirmation set. Failed families or seeds are never silently removed to improve scores. Ordinary finite bad predictions remain in every risk calculation.

## Twelve-week execution and a concrete generality gate

Use AdamW, batch 16 origins, 10,000 updates, weight decay $10^{-4}$, and learning-rate candidates $\{10^{-4},3\times 10^{-4},10^{-3}\}$ with tuning seed 5. Select each model's configuration on validation before fresh final seeds. Seven models × (three tuning + five final fits) gives 56 principal fits across the mixed linear/nonlinear population. Pilot profiling must fit these within 80 accelerator-hours; reserve 20 for references/sensitivities and 20 for the conditional contact replication, with a 120-hour ceiling. If the registry cannot fit, revise the protocol before confirmation instead of dropping difficult baselines. Software version, family manifests, selected hyperparameters, score sample count, numerical tolerances, and analysis script hashes are mandatory lock fields.

Weeks 1–2 validate the generator, initial states, cloning, and pilot budget. Weeks 3–5 establish identification and snapshot references. Weeks 6–8 fit the frozen registry; weeks 9–10 open confirmation once; weeks 11–12 analyze and write. No human acquisition or learned active probing is required.

A broader learning claim additionally requires a gated replication on the official [dm_control planar walker](https://github.com/google-deepmind/dm_control/blob/main/dm_control/suite/walker.py), whose [model defines six bilateral actuators](https://github.com/google-deepmind/dm_control/blob/main/dm_control/suite/walker.xml). Pin its release. Change only the left/right knee gains to $1\pm b$, with $\lvert b\rvert\in [0.05,0.2]$; retain contacts. Use 40 Hz records: eight generalized positions excluding global forward translation, nine velocities, and total vertical foot force (`[time,18]`), six controls, and `[20,19]` future targets containing positions, velocities, and two labeled foot forces. The endpoint is the signed difference in half-second foot impulses normalized by nominal body weight times horizon.

Prepare bounded open-loop schedules on a separate nominal body during the pilot, then freeze them before target-body generation. They remain known at forecast origin; future feedback from the target body is unavailable. Compare C, the cart-selected learned B, a snapshot control, and an equation-aware fit on independent body families, with full-history/recent-window audits repeated. Do not assume exact ambiguity or a valid freeze gate transfers. The week-2 gate requires stable reference schedules, verified side transformation/contact extraction, and feasibility within the reserved compute; otherwise this extension is omitted and the paper makes no general dynamics-method claim. Contact results are exploratory replication, not an automatic extension of the cart significance test.

## Relation to prior work and claim limits

[Chirality Nets](https://arxiv.org/abs/1911.00029) and [SWAP](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.19928) establish equivariant recurrence; [RMA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2107.04034) and [VariBAD](https://arxiv.org/abs/1910.08348) establish context adaptation. [V-JEPA 2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.09985), [Identifiable World Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.07028), and [Compact but Moving](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21787) require a narrow claim about this controlled retention test. A consistent benefit beyond snapshot and identification controls would justify a useful memory finding. A cart-only or null result remains a bounded diagnostic contribution, with novelty judged accordingly.
